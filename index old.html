DDC Cooler Welding Time Study

Direct-observation time study of 12 heat exchanger coolers on AKG's production floor in Mebane, NC. Decomposed the welding cycle into 4 steps, identifying TIG welding as the primary bottleneck with the widest variance (5.9 to 14.7 min) , driven by core edge fit, robot vs hand switching, and operator variability. Surfaced that Steps 1 and 4 are invisible to SAP and job tickets, a key gap in production data coverage.

Robots in Assembly Lines

Built an integer programming model in GAMS to solve the Assembly Line Balancing Problem (ALBP) integrating additive manufacturing and robotics. Modeled 18 tasks across hybrid human-robot vs human-only configurations at two cycle times. Key finding: robots save 1 workstation only under tight cycle time constraints (20s cycle: 5 vs 6 stations). At 30s cycle time, both converge to 4 stations , showing robots are only worth the capital at high throughput demand.
